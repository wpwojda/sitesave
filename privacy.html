Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026

Sitesave is a visual bookmarking tool for designers. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user. We have tried to keep this as plain and honest as possible.

Who we are

Sitesave is an independent product. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, you can contact us at the address at the bottom of this page.

What data we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your email address and name, via Google Sign-In
  • Your Google profile picture (used to display your avatar)

When you use Sitesave, we store:

  • The URLs you save to your collection
  • The names, tags, and colours you assign to saved sites
  • The date and time each site was saved
  • Whether you have marked a site as a favourite

We do not collect any payment information, browsing history, or any data beyond what is listed above.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the Sitesave service:

  • To identify you when you sign in
  • To store and display your saved collection
  • To sync your collection across devices

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use your data for advertising.

Where your data is stored

Your account and collection data is stored using Supabase, a database platform hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). This means your data is stored within the EU and subject to EU data protection standards.

Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth in combination with Google OAuth. When you sign in with Google, Google processes your login credentials and returns a secure token to Sitesave. We do not receive or store your Google password.

Cookies and local storage

Sitesave uses browser local storage (not cookies) to store two things:

  • Your authentication session, so you remain signed in between visits
  • Your light or dark mode preference

These are strictly necessary for the service to function. They are not used for tracking or advertising. No third-party tracking or analytics cookies are set by Sitesave.

Google's OAuth sign-in process may set cookies on Google's own domains as part of their standard authentication flow. These are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Object — object to how we process your data

You can delete your account and all saved data at any time from within the app — click your profile avatar and select Delete account. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

For any other data requests, contact us using the details below.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data — including your saved collection and account credentials — is permanently deleted from our systems.

Third-party services

Sitesave uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (their privacy policy)
  • Google OAuth — sign-in (their privacy policy)
  • Thum.io — website screenshot generation. URLs you save are sent to Thum.io to generate preview images (their website)
  • Google Favicon Service — used to display website icons

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Sitesave after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise your data rights, please get in touch at privacy@sitesave.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days as required by UK GDPR.
