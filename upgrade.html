Common questions
What happens when my year is up?
Your library stays exactly as it is. You can still view, organise and share your saves — you just can't add new ones until you renew. If you've deleted saves and dropped below 50, you can save again up to the free limit.
What if I renew while I still have days left?
Your new year starts after your current one ends. No days are wasted.
Is this a subscription?
No. It's a single payment for one year of Pro access. Nothing auto-renews. You decide if and when to pay again.
What currency is this in?
Payments are in USD. Your card provider will convert at their current rate. Tax is included in the price shown.
Can I get a refund?
All sales are final. If you run into a technical problem, get in touch at support@sitesave.co.uk and we'll sort it out.
