Total users
Total registered accounts. Try 500 or 1,000 to see when costs start to kick in.
Paid users
How many users have actually paid for Pro. SaaS benchmarks: 2–5% of total users convert. Revenue is: paid users × Pro price.
Avg saves / user / month
New saves per user per month — drives Thum.io API usage. Your current avg is shown in the metrics above. Casual: 2–5. Power users: 10–20+.
Cache hit rate (%)
% of saves where R2 already has the screenshot — no Thum.io call needed. Early stage: ~25%. Mature product: 60–70%. Higher = cheaper.
Pro price (yearly, £)
Yearly payment for Pro. Revenue = paid users × this price. For yearly view, this is total revenue assuming all paid users converted within that year.
Screenshot size (KB)
Average Thum.io JPG size in R2. Currently ~140KB at 1200px width / 900 crop. Affects R2 and DB storage estimates — check your R2 bucket to calibrate.
