Total users
Total registered accounts. Model growth by typing an exact number or sliding. Try 500 or 1,000 to see when costs start to kick in.
Paid users
How many users have actually paid for Pro. Set this to your real current number (you can see it above in the "At limit" metric as a proxy). SaaS benchmarks suggest 2–5% of total users will convert — at 100 users that's 2–5 paid. Revenue is: paid users × Pro price.
Avg saves / user / month
New saves per user per month — drives Thum.io API usage. Your current avg is shown in the metrics above. Casual users: 2–5. Power users: 10–20+.
Cache hit rate
% of saves where R2 already has the screenshot from a previous user saving the same URL — no Thum.io call needed. Early stage: ~25%. Mature product with hundreds of users: 60–70%. Higher = cheaper.
Pro price (one-time, £)
One-time payment for Pro. Revenue = paid users × this price. For yearly view, this is total revenue in a year assuming all paid users converted within that year.
Screenshot size (KB)
Average Thum.io JPG size in R2. Currently ~325KB at 500px width. Affects R2 storage cost estimate only — check your R2 bucket to calibrate.
